About me

A profile built at the crossroads

Two schools, three countries, one clear direction — toward AI systems that are both rigorous and meaningful.

My path started in Bordeaux with two intense years of classes préparatoires in Maths and Physics. That foundation launched me to Grenoble INP, where I'm completing a double degree that most engineers never attempt: a Diplôme d'Ingénieur from ENSE3 in Intelligent Systems, coupled with an M2 in Artificial Intelligence from ENSIMAG.

The combination is unusual — and intentional. Systems engineers know how hardware constraints shape every algorithm. AI researchers know how to push those algorithms further. I operate in both worlds, and I believe that's where the most interesting problems live: at the boundary between the two.

"My INRIA internship placed me at the intersection of pure numerical mathematics and high-performance computing — exactly the kind of challenge that sharpens both sides of that double profile."

After a semester at McMaster University in Canada 🇨🇦, a research stint at INRIA, and now designing LLM pipelines at Thales Alenia Space, I'm as comfortable writing CUDA kernels as I am structuring a document analysis chain for industrial decision support.

Outside the terminal: I play rugby at the first division level (R1) with GUC SMH in Grenoble, I hike in the mountains whenever the weekends allow, and I was VP of the ENSE3 Rugby Association for two years. I think the best engineers are trained by adversity — on the pitch as much as in the debugger.
